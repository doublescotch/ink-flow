## Name

Ink Flow - VTK CCXL Tattoo Loader

## Summary (250 characters max)

Turn every VTK tattoo archive you own into stackable "Ink 1 / Ink 2" slots in the character creator,
ripperdoc and
mirror. Body, arms and head. A small builder runs on your PC; nothing from other authors is
redistributed.

## Category

Modders Resources and Tutorials → no. **Character Customization** (or "Tools").

## Description

[size=5][b]What it does[/b][/size]

VTK tattoos normally replace the vanilla body tattoo texture: one tattoo file, one look, and two
tattoo mods fight
for the same slot. Ink Flow adds its own slots to the character creator ("Ink 1", "Ink 2", … as many
as you want), and
each slot lists [b]every[/b] VTK tattoo archive you own. Pick one per slot, stack them, change them
at the ripperdoc or
at the apartment mirror. Body, left arm, right arm and head tattoos are all handled, and a tattoo
that only covers the
head or one arm stays on that part.

Because the tattoos are yours (the archives you already downloaded), Ink Flow ships [b]no
tattoo[/b]. It ships a small
Windows builder: you drop your tattoo archives in, give each one the name you want to read in the
creator, click Build,
click Install. The result is one [b]Ink_Flow.archive[/b] + [b].xl[/b] in your game, built on your
machine from your files.

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Cyberpunk 2077 2.x (PC). Built and tested on 2.5.
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/4198]ArchiveXL[/url] and its requirements
(RED4ext, Redscript).
[*]The [url=https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0]Microsoft .NET 10 runtime[/url] (x64):
needed by WolvenKit
Console, which the builder downloads from GitHub on first run (45 MB, version pinned and checked).
[*]Female V. A body mod of the VTK family (vanilla body, Angel, VTK Vanilla HD Head…) or the vanilla
body.
[/list]

[size=5][b]How to use[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Unzip, run [b]InkFlowBuilder.exe[/b]. The Setup box shows four lines: game folder, ArchiveXL,
.NET 10, WolvenKit
Console. Fix what is red (the buttons do it: Download, open the Microsoft page).
[*][b]Add files…[/b] or [b]Add folder…[/b] with your VTK tattoo archives (the .archive files you
would normally put in
archive\pc\mod). The list shows what each file covers: Body, Arm L, Arm R, Head. Files Ink Flow
cannot use are greyed
out with the reason.
[*]Double-click a name to rename it (20 characters max, that is what you read in the creator).
Uncheck what you do not
want.
[*]Choose the number of slots and whether the slots also appear at the apartment mirror.
[*][b]Build[/b] (about two minutes for 18 tattoos the first time, less afterwards), then [b]Install
into game[/b] with
the game closed. If the original tattoo archives are still in archive\pc\mod the builder tells you
and can move them
to a backup folder (they would paint a permanent tattoo under the slots).
[*]In game: character creator, ripperdoc, mirror → "Ink 1", "Ink 2" under Body Tattoos.
[/list]

Add tattoos later? Add the files, Build, Install. Your character keeps the tattoos already chosen in
each slot (a
tattoo keeps its internal id across rebuilds, even if you rename the file).

[b]Changed your body mod?[/b] Build again. The shells that carry the tattoos are copies of the body
and head meshes
that were active when you built.

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]

For each tattoo file the builder extracts the texture(s), copies your active body / arm / head
meshes with their skin
materials replaced by the vanilla tattoo decal material, points the decal at the tattoo texture, and
writes one
appearance per tattoo and per slot plus the character-creator entries (CCXL). The head shell carries
a copy of the
face morph target, so it follows face sculpting. The textures are copied byte for byte: what you see
is the author's
tattoo, unchanged. Everything is generated by WolvenKit Console from JSON; the source of the builder
is public.

[size=5][b]Known limits (1.0)[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Female V only.
[*]VTK family only. UV Framework tattoos (Solo-type bodies) are listed as "ignored" for now.
[*]No first-person shells: the tattoos show in third person, photo mode, mirror and inventory, not
on the arms seen
in first person.
[*]Text options in the creator, no icons yet.
[*]Face sculpting changed at the ripperdoc after a build has not been QA'd (it follows the morphs of
an already sculpted
face).
[/list]

[size=5][b]Antivirus[/b][/size]

The builder is a single-file PyInstaller executable (Python + tkinter). Some antivirus heuristics
flag such files.
The SHA-256 of the exe is in the file description; the source is on GitHub and you can run it with
Python instead
([i]python InkFlowBuilder.py[/i]).
